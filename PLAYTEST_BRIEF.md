# Selvedge — playtest brief (round 4)

A rebuild, not a tweak. Read the rules page first; it opens on its own, and it's also under **Menu → Rules**.

## What changed since round 3
- **No pattern book.** Every cloth carries its own order: a tag of four chips under it, the colours its
  customer wants on its four edges, on any sides. When a cloth's edges match its tag at inspection, it ships
  and leaves your deck. Ship 13 of the 18 within three seasons.
- **Dyes mix.** Edges start in the cloth's own colour. Cloths that touch mix their colours into the edges
  they share: red beside blue makes purple on both. A third colour turns an edge brown. Too many edges of a
  colour, or any brown, spoils a cloth for good.
- **Cloths turn.** After choosing a peg, press **Turn it** to choose which way the cloth faces.
- **A smaller rack** (3 × 3). Only about half your cloths come out each season.
- **The basket is behind a button again** ("See the basket"), not on the board.
