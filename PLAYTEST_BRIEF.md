# Selvedge — playtest brief (round 3)

Same game as round 2, with what you asked for. The rules page opens first, and it's also under
**Menu → Rules**.

## What changed since round 2
- **Set a cloth aside.** Pick a cloth, then press **Set aside for good**: it leaves the game, and you draw
  another.
- **The basket is on the board**, under your hand, sorted by colour. You can see what's left to draw; the
  draw order is still hidden. (It was always available behind the "in basket" count, but that didn't look
  tappable.)
- **Hanging always takes the "Hang it" button.** Tapping the same peg twice no longer hangs, so a double tap
  can't place a cloth by accident.
