# Selvedge — playtest brief (round 5): your workshop, v10

This is your design, built as directly as I could. The rules page opens first, and it's also under
**Menu → Rules**.

## Changed after your first look
- **Smooth dye edges.** No more pixels: each spray is stored as an exact shape, so a turned cloth leaves
  a clean diagonal line.
- **Move cloths after spraying** to pull them apart and see what each one got.
- **9 cloths, exactly the quilt's size.** Every cloth you dye ends up in the quilt.

## What's there
- **9 blank cloths, 4 drawn per round, 8 rounds.**
- **The bench:**
  - tap a drawn cloth to lay it down;
  - drag it to move it (snaps to a grid);
  - turn it in 15° steps;
  - put it on top, or take it off.
  Overlap freely.
- **Spray** red, yellow or blue, once per round. Whatever you can see from above takes the dye.
  Covered parts stay as they were. Dyes mix (orange, green, purple; all three make brown).
- **The quilt:** 3 × 3. Select a drawn cloth, **Sew into the quilt**, tap a square, turn it, and **Sew it in**.
  You see the points it would add before you commit.
- **Scoring (first pass):**
  - each seam is checked at 12 stitches, 1 point for each where the colours match;
  - +5 for each square showing 3 or more colours.
- **Selvedge v8** is still there at https://poeticmatter.github.io/mordant/#v8.

## Choices I made where your brief left room
Squares only (no rectangles yet), one spray per round, a free choice of dye, a hand of 4, 8 rounds. All
easy to change.
