# Selvedge — playtest brief (round 7): the puzzle

Your direction, built as stated. The rules page opens first, and it's also under **Menu → Rules**.

## What it is
- **4 cloths, no basket.** Each cloth has its own objective, shown on its card as a picture beside the cloth
  itself.
- **Objectives say "include this":** one or two simple regions in a colour, from:
  - half;
  - diagonal half;
  - quarter;
  - corner;
  - centre;
  - edge stripe;
  - middle band.
  Bare cloth counts as a colour. Hatched means "anything". The picture can be turned or flipped any
  way. A region counts at 90 %, and the card shows the current % until it's done.
- **Minigolf scoring.** Each spray is a stroke. **Par 3**, one spray of each colour, in an order the puzzle
  decides. Every puzzle came from a semi-random game I played to par, so par is always reachable. Every
  dye is asked for somewhere, so you can't beat it by skipping a colour.
- **Cloths off the bench are safe** from the spray, so a finished cloth can sit out.
- **Start over** any time. **Next puzzle** after solving. **Menu → New game** takes a puzzle number if you
  want to replay one.

## What I made up where your brief left room
- Three of the four cloths ask for two features, in different colours. One asks for a single feature,
  because the top cloth of the last spray always ends up one solid colour. That cloth tends to be the
  natural stencil.
- Generator layouts snap to halves and to 45° turns near the centre, so the regions stay simple.
