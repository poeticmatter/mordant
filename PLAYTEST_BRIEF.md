# Selvedge — playtest brief (round 6): commissions

Your workshop, aimed at patterned sheets instead of a quilt. The rules page opens first, and it's also under
**Menu → Rules**.

## What changed
- **No colour mixing.** A spray covers whatever was there, like screen printing. Layering order is the
  whole game now.
- **No quilt.** Each game deals **4 random commissions**, shown as pictures at the top:
  - **3 quilt blocks** in named colours, any way round. Bare cloth counts as a colour. The blocks are:
    - half-square triangle;
    - hourglass;
    - square in a square;
    - four-patch;
    - diamond in a square;
    - cross;
    - three bars.
  - **1 other:**
    - *mostly one colour* (at least half);
    - *mirror image*;
    - or *kept apart* (two dyes that never touch).
- **Select a cloth and every open commission shows its match % and stars, live.** Tap a commission to
  deliver (you confirm first). The cloth leaves the game.
- **Stars:** 70 % = ★, 85 % = ★★, 95 % = ★★★. Out of 12.
- **6 cloths, 4 drawn a round, 8 rounds.** Cloths you don't deliver are your stencils, and they take dye
  too.
