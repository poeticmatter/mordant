# Mordant — Rules v4

Identical to [v3](RULES_v3.md) except:

- **(v4) One stripe per season.** A cloth soaks at most one stripe each season: from the first neighbour
  hung beside it *after* it. From then on it is *soaked* and ignores further bleeds until the next season
  starts. The wet cloth being hung never soaks anything itself.

Consequence: a cloth's memory is a biography written one line per season. A full three-stripe order
can't be filled before the end of season 2, and which neighbour touches a cloth *first* is the
whole decision.
