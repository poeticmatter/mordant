# Selvedge — Rules v10 "Workshop" (playtest round 5)

The playtester's design. v10 = v9 with exact dye shapes and a 9-cloth deck. Lay cloths on a bench, overlapping and turned, and spray them.
Whatever lies on top resists the dye. Sew finished cloths into a quilt.

## Components
- **9 cloths**, blank, one per quilt square. Each is 12 units square. Dye only ever builds up.
- **Bench**: 36 × 36 units. A cloth's centre must lie on the bench.
- **Quilt**: 3 × 3 squares.

## Colours
Red + yellow = orange, yellow + blue = green, red + blue = purple. All three = brown.

## A round (8 rounds)
1. Shuffle the deck and draw 4.
2. **Lay out** any of the drawn cloths on the bench: positions snap to whole units, rotation to 15° steps.
   A cloth put down later lies on top. You can rearrange freely until you spray.
3. **Spray** one dye (once per round, optional). Every part of a cloth not covered by a cloth above it takes
   the dye, exactly to the covering cloth's edge. Afterwards cloths can be moved again to look at them.
4. **Sew** any drawn cloth into an empty quilt square, at any quarter turn, at any time in the round. It
   stays there for good.
5. **End the round**: drawn cloths not in the quilt go back into the deck with their patterns.

## End and score
The game ends when the quilt is full or after round 8.
- **Seams:** each seam has 12 evenly spaced stitches; 1 point per stitch where both sides share a colour.
  Undyed and brown never count.
- **Colourful squares:** 5 points per square showing at least 3 different dyed colours (not counting brown),
  each covering at least 2 % of it.
