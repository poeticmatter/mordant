# Mordant — Rules v5 (playtest round 1)

Complete rules. Changes from v4 are marked **(v5)**.

## Components
- **18 cloths**: 6 start Red, 6 Yellow, 6 Blue (one stripe each). Each has a name.
- **Stripe memory.** A cloth remembers its last 3 stripes, oldest at the top, newest at the hem.
  A 4th stripe pushes the oldest out.
- **(v5) Kin memory.** A cloth remembers, for the whole game, every cloth it has shared dye with
  (bled into, or been bled into by).
- **Drying rack**: 4 × 3 pegs.
- **Order deck (54)**, 8 face up:
  | Order | Wants | Points | Copies |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Sequence | one cloth whose stripes read e.g. Blue · Yellow · Red | 4 | 1 of each of the 27 |
  | Ending | one cloth ending in e.g. Yellow · Red | 2 | 2 of each of the 9 |
  | **(v5) Bolt** | two kin cloths, both ending in e.g. Yellow · Red | 6 | 1 of each of the 9 |

## A season
1. Shuffle every cloth you own into the basket. Draw 3.
2. **Hang** a cloth from your hand on an empty peg.
3. **Bleed:** it adds its newest stripe to each orthogonal neighbour that hasn't soaked yet this season.
   Each soaked neighbour is now *soaked* for the season, and becomes kin with the wet cloth.
4. Draw 1. Repeat until the rack is full or your hand is empty.
5. **Inspection:** orders take matching cloths (best total). Shipped cloths score and leave forever.
6. **Withdrawal:** an order unfilled after its 2nd inspection leaves. Empty order slots refill.
7. Unsold cloths return to the basket with their stripes and kin.

## End
After 4 seasons. **Target: 40 points.**
