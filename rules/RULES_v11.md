# Selvedge — Rules v11 "Workshop: commissions" (playtest round 6)

The playtester's workshop, with patterned sheets as the goal. Lay cloths on a bench, overlapping and turned, and
spray them. Whatever lies on top keeps the dye off. Deliver finished cloths to commissions that ask for
particular patterns.

## Components
- **6 cloths**, blank, 12 units square.
- **Bench**: 36 × 36 units. A cloth's centre must lie on it.
- **4 commissions**, dealt at random from the seed: 3 different quilt blocks in named colours, plus one
  of "mostly one colour", "mirror image" or "kept apart".

## Colour
Red, yellow and blue, plus bare cloth. A spray **covers** whatever colour was there; dyes don't mix.

## A round (8 rounds)
1. Shuffle the deck and draw 4.
2. **Lay out** any drawn cloths on the bench: positions snap to whole units, rotation to 15° steps. A cloth
   put down later lies on top.
3. **Spray** one dye (once per round, optional). Every part of a cloth not covered by a cloth above it takes
   the dye. Afterwards cloths can be moved again to look at them.
4. **Deliver** any drawn cloth to an open commission, at any time in the round. It's scored, and the cloth
   leaves the game.
5. **End the round**: undelivered cloths go back into the deck with their patterns.

## Commissions
A cloth is judged on a 24 × 24 grid of sample points, as a match from 0 to 100 %.
- **Quilt blocks** (half-square triangle, hourglass, square in a square, four-patch, diamond in a square,
  cross, three bars). Match = the share of points that have the colour the block wants there, under the best
  of the 8 rotations and reflections. A point exactly on a block's edge may match either side.
- **Mostly one colour:** that dye covers at least 50 %. Match = share ÷ 50 %, capped at 100 %.
- **Mirror image:** the best match against a reflection across the vertical, horizontal or either diagonal
  line, scaled down unless a second colour covers 15 % or more.
- **Kept apart:** two named dyes. Scaled down unless each covers 20 %, and down again for every pair of
  neighbouring sample points where they touch (no credit left at 24 touching pairs).

**Stars:** 70 % ★, 85 % ★★, 95 % ★★★. Score = total stars, out of 12. The game ends when every commission is
delivered or after round 8.
